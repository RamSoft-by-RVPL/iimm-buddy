People who can post

Class reps (CR), mess secretaries and hostel secretaries get a "Post an update" screen in the app (0.8.20 or newer). Their posts start with their role ("CR: ...") and show under Notifications with their name. At most 10 posts a day each. A CR can also cancel and put back classes of their batch.

Polls

A question with 2 to 6 answers. Phones get a notification and vote in the app (0.8.21 or newer); one vote per phone, which can be changed while the poll is open. CRs and secretaries can make polls from the app too.

Files from secretaries

Teacher emails

Students see an Email button next to a teacher's name in the app (0.8.21 or newer). One teacher per line: name, then the address. Only @iimmumbai.ac.in addresses.

Academic calendar

Upload the calendar document (Word, .docx). Its exams, holidays, breaks and term dates go into every student's timetable and month view, and the holiday planner stops counting classes on those days. You see the dates first; nothing changes until you press Publish.

On the server

Exam timetable

Each student sees the papers of their own courses, with a countdown and reminders the evening before and an hour before. To publish one, give the exam schedule to Claude.

Mess menu

The week's menu shown in the app, for everyone. To publish one, give the mess's menu file to Claude: it reads the file and sends the menu here.
