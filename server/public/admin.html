IIMM Buddy admin

Sign in

Set your login Needs the publisher key once. After this you sign in with the username and password you choose here.
Publish the week's timetable

Choose the Excel sheet from the programme office's mail. You see the classes first; nothing reaches the students until you press Publish.

Academic calendar

Upload the calendar document (Word, .docx). Its exams, holidays, breaks and term dates go into every student's timetable and month view, and the holiday planner stops counting classes on those days. You see the dates first; nothing changes until you press Publish.

Cancel a class

Pick the day, tick the classes that are off and press Cancel classes. Students' phones mark them Cancelled on the timetable, stop their reminders, leave them out of attendance and say "Class cancelled". Put back undoes it. (The app also spots classes that disappear from the portal by itself.)

Mess menu

The week's menu shown in the app, for everyone. To publish one, give the mess's menu file to Claude: it reads the file and sends the menu here.

Exam timetable

Each student sees the papers of their own courses, with a countdown and reminders the evening before and an hour before. To publish one, give the exam schedule to Claude.

Send a notification

Pushed to phones at once (app version 1.5 or newer); older versions get it at their next check. A message set for later goes out when the first phone checks in after that time, usually within a few minutes.

Only these people (optional): pick one or more, holding Ctrl or Cmd for several. Leave none picked to send to the batch or everyone. Needs app version 0.8.8 or newer on their phone.
