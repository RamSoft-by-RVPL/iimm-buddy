What it does

Everything you check ten times a day, in one place

No more opening the attendance portal, Moodle and your mail one after another. The app pulls it all together and tells you what needs doing.

Attendance that does the maths

Every course with its percentage, how many classes you can still miss, and how many you must attend to get back to 85%.

A reminder before every class

"Class in 5 mins" with the course and the room, straight from the app, even when it's closed. You choose how early.

Moodle notes on your phone

PDFs and slides download and open inside the app. You get a notification when something new is posted, and you can search inside the files.

Deadlines you won't miss

Assignment reminders start two days before the due time, then as often as you like. It also shows what you've already submitted.

The full week's timetable

Week and month views, including classes that aren't on the portal yet, plus holidays and exams from the academic calendar.

Plan leave without the risk

Finds long weekends and the longest breaks you can take, and checks any dates you pick against every course.

Notifications

Know before it matters, not after

The app checks in the background and tells you only what's worth knowing.

  • Before every class. Tap it and the timetable opens.
  • When an absence is marked or a course drops below 85%.
  • When new material lands on Moodle. Tap it to open that course.
  • When the timetable or calendar changes, the moment it's updated.

Install

Up and running in two minutes

The app isn't on the Play Store yet, so you install it from this page. It's a normal Android app and updates itself from here.

Download

Tap Download for Android and open the file when it finishes.

Allow the install

If your phone asks, let your browser install apps. This is only needed once.

Sign in

Allow notifications, then sign in with your institute Microsoft account. Your courses appear in a few seconds.

Chrome says the file "might be harmful"? It says that about every app that doesn't come from the Play Store. Tap Download anyway. Already have the app? It shows an Update button when a new version is out, so you don't need this page again.

Questions

Good to know

Is my password safe?

You type it only on Microsoft's own sign-in page, the same one the attendance portal and Moodle use. The app never sees or stores your password. It only keeps the sign-in session on your phone, like a browser does.

Is this an official IIM Mumbai app?

No. It's made by a student for students. It reads the same attendance portal and Moodle you already use. If the app and the portal ever disagree, the portal is right.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free and has no ads.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. It's Android only for now.

Will it drain my battery?

No. It checks in the background a few times an hour, which uses very little. For class reminders to arrive on time, allow the app to run without battery limits when it asks.

What does it collect?

Your attendance and Moodle data stay on your phone. To fix problems, the app sends a short log to its server: your roll number and name, app version, and whether syncs worked. You can turn this off in Settings, and the full privacy policy is in the app.
